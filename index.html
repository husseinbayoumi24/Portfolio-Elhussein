01 — ABOUT ME

A Data Analyst With a Business Mindset.

I'm ElHussein Bayoumi, a Commerce student specializing in Accounting and building my professional career around Data Analysis and Business Intelligence.

My goal is not simply to create charts. I focus on the complete analytical process: understanding the business question, preparing the data, cleaning inconsistencies, analyzing patterns, calculating KPIs, building dashboards, and communicating insights clearly enough to support a real decision.

My background in accounting gives me a strong interest in revenue, cost, profit, margins, customers, sales performance, financial reporting, and business operations. I combine that business understanding with Excel, SQL, Power BI and Python.

I enjoy working with messy datasets and turning them into structured information. I have practiced data cleaning, multi-table analysis, KPI development, dashboard design, exploratory analysis, SQL querying, and business-focused reporting.

I am continuously developing my technical and professional skills with a long-term objective of becoming a highly capable Data Analyst / BI professional who can understand both the numbers and the business behind them.
